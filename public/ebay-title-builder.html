eBay Title Builder: the 80-Character Formula for Auto Parts

eBay gives you 80 characters per title. For auto parts, most sellers waste 20 of them. Here's the structure that eBay's Cassini search engine actually rewards — and a free tool that applies it for you.

The 4-block formula

Part Type → OEM / Interchange Number → Fitment → Placement / Piece Count

Every block matches a real buyer search pattern: "control arm" matches the category keyword, "K643168" matches part-number searches (the highest-intent query type on eBay Motors), "2015-2020 Ford F-150" matches vehicle fitment searches, and "2pc" answers the quantity question before a buyer clicks.

Five rules that prevent wasted characters

  1. Never invent a part number. A wrong interchange number gets clicks, then returns. Every number in a title must exist verbatim in your supplier data.
  2. One fitment range per title. "Fits F150 F250 Expedition Navigator" reads like spam and truncates. Pick the primary vehicle; list the rest in the fitment table.
  3. No promotional words. FREE SHIPPING, HOT, L@@K, BEST — zero ranking value, pure character waste.
  4. American side naming. Write "Left (Driver Side)" only if space allows; otherwise "Left" — US buyers understand Driver = Left.
  5. Test truncation. eBay truncates titles in mobile search around 55–60 visible characters. Your part type and number must land inside the first 50.

FAQ

What is the best structure for an eBay Motors title?
Part Type + OEM/Interchange Part Number + Fitment (Year-Make-Model) + Placement or Piece Count. Example: Front Lower Control Arm K643168 for 2015-2020 Ford F-150, 2pc — 74 characters.
Why is the 80-character limit important on eBay?
eBay titles are capped at 80 characters and every keyword beyond the limit is wasted. Titles stuffed past relevance lose impressions for high-intent searches like part numbers.
Should I put the part number in the eBay title?
Yes for auto parts. Buyers frequently search by OEM or interchange part number, and the exact number in the title is one of the strongest relevance signals on eBay Motors.
How many promotional words should an eBay title have?
None. Words like FREE SHIPPING or HOT do not improve search ranking and consume characters that real keywords need.
