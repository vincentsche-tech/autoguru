eBay Motors Item Specifics: the Field-by-Field Guide

Item Specifics are how eBay Motors buyers filter — and how Cassini decides whether your listing appears when they do. Fill them wrong and you vanish from filtered search. Invent them and you risk VERO complaints. Here's the field-by-field playbook for unbranded auto parts.

MPN vs Interchange is the #1 mistake for dropshippers: the OEM number in your supplier title belongs in Interchange Part Number, while MPN is Does Not Apply for unbranded parts.

Why blanks cost more than you think

When a buyer filters by "Placement on Vehicle: Front Left" or searches an interchange number, eBay matches against Item Specifics — not your description text. A listing with empty specifics is invisible to every filtered browse session, which on Motors is most of them. Completed listings also get a relevance boost: eBay has stated listings with complete specifics rank better in best match.

The VERO trap for aftermarket sellers

eBay's Verified Rights Owner program lets brands take down listings that misuse their trademarks. The classic trigger: putting a vehicle brand's OEM number in the Brand or MPN field of an unbranded part. The number itself is fine — where you put it matters. Correct pattern:

  • Brand: Unbranded
  • MPN: Does Not Apply (or the aftermarket manufacturer's number if one exists)
  • Interchange Part Number: all OEM numbers — this is the legally safe home for them
  • Title: "…Replacement for Ford F-150" — compatible-with phrasing, never "Genuine Ford"

FAQ

What are Item Specifics on eBay?
Structured fields (Brand, MPN, Interchange Part Number, Placement on Vehicle, Material, Type) that describe your listing to eBay's search engine. They power filter navigation and are a major Cassini ranking input.
What MPN should I use for unbranded aftermarket parts?
'Does Not Apply'. Never copy a vehicle brand's OEM number into MPN — that misrepresents the brand and risks a VERO complaint.
What is the difference between MPN and Interchange Part Number?
MPN is the number assigned by the manufacturer of the item you sell. Interchange lists the OEM numbers the part replaces. For aftermarket parts, OEM numbers belong in Interchange.
Which Item Specifics matter most for eBay Motors ranking?
Brand, MPN, Interchange Part Number, Placement on Vehicle and Type drive the most buyer-filter traffic. Blank fields remove you from filtered results entirely.
What if my supplier data is missing a value?
Leave it blank or use Does Not Apply where allowed. Never guess: a wrong Interchange number causes returns, a wrong Brand can trigger IP complaints.
