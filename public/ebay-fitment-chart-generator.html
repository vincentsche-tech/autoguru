The exclusion problem (where returns are born)

Supplier application lists usually say what a part fits. They rarely shout what it doesn't — and that's exactly where compatibility returns come from. Real exclusions we see in distributor data every week:

  • Not fit 3.0L engine — engine-size exclusions inside an otherwise clean year range
  • Automatic transmission only — drivetrain exclusions buried in a footnote
  • Not for models built after 03/2011 — production-date cutoffs, not model-year cutoffs
  • Excludes Mexico-built models — assembly-plant exclusions that no buyer guesses
⚠ Exclusions must appear in the fitment chart, not just buried in the description text. A buyer scanning the table sees "fits my truck" and orders; the exclusion line is your only defense.

Fitment vs. eBay's My Garage

Beyond the description table, eBay supports structured compatibility (parts compatibility) through catalog data. For unbranded and aftermarket parts the catalog often can't match, which is exactly why the in-description fitment chart matters — it's the only compatibility signal a buyer gets for interchange parts. Keep the chart complete even when you fill eBay's compatibility fields.

FAQ

What is a fitment chart on eBay?
A compatibility table listing the years, makes, models and trims a part fits. eBay Motors buyers rely on it to confirm a part works with their vehicle, and My Garage matches listings against compatibility data.
How do I create a fitment table for my eBay listing?
Parse the application list from your supplier data into Year / Make / Model / Trim columns and paste the table into your description. The EbayAutoGuru generator does this automatically from any data package.
Why do fitment mistakes cause returns on eBay Motors?
Auto parts are the most return-prone eBay category because a part that almost fits is still unusable. Missing exclusions — production-date cutoffs, engine-size limits, assembly-plant exclusions — are the most common cause.
Should fitment go in the title or the description?
Both, at different detail levels. The title carries the primary vehicle; the fitment chart carries every application, trim level and exclusion.
